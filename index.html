Who we are

Hashtag SA Unite is a community-driven non-profit that brings people together to spark positive action across South Africa.

We work with young people, families, the elderly and low-income households. Our programs run from community centers in Gauteng and the Western Cape and through mobile outreach units.

Community leaders, organizations and businesses join us because they care about collaboration, integrity and lasting change. Together we are building better communities for our people and our children. You can read about our programs on the Services page.

We do our work to help those who are in need. This is to build better communities for our people and our children.
