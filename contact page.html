Frequently asked questions

Who can ask for support?

We work with young people aged 15 to 25, families and caregivers, the elderly, and low-income families.

Where do your programs run?

Our programs run from community centers across Gauteng and the Western Cape, and through mobile outreach units that reach local township centers. Get in touch first and we will help you find the right place.

When is the office open?

Monday to Thursday: 9:00 to 17:00
Friday: 9:00 to 14:30

How can my business or organization partner with you?

Send us a message on the enquiry form and choose "Donation or partnership", or email info@hashtagsaunite.co.za.
