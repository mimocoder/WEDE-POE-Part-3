Brand story

Hashtag SA Unite was founded to bring people together and spark positive action. It began with a vision to create a connected community where voice, purpose, and shared goals drive real impact across South Africa.

Everything we do starts with people: neighbors, families, young people and leaders who want to see their communities grow stronger.

Mission and values

Our mission is to unite individuals and communities through shared purpose, collaboration, and empowerment. We value:

  • Integrity: we act honestly and are open about what we do.
  • Inclusivity: everyone is welcome and everyone has a voice.
  • Unity: we achieve more when we work together.
  • Meaningful change: we focus on actions that make a lasting difference.

Who we work with

Our work is for passionate individuals, community leaders, organizations, and businesses who care about collaboration, progress, and making a lasting positive impact in South Africa.

The people we serve include young people aged 15 to 25, families and caregivers, the elderly, and low-income families.

Where we work

Our programs run from community centers across Gauteng and the Western Cape, and through mobile outreach units that reach local township centers. Our office is at 13 Parow St, Parow, Cape Town.

See the map on our Contact page.

How you can get involved

Individuals, community leaders, organizations and businesses are welcome to partner with us. If you or your family need support, we would like to hear from you. You can also read about our programs on the Services page.
